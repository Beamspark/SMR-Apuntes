<h1 style="color: #ab47bc;">🤝 Unit 10 — Chatting Across Cultures</h1>

La realización de presentaciones personales profesionales, la interacción fluida en intercambios sociales y laborales respondiendo a saludos formales e informales, y la redacción precisa del perfil profesional (*Personal Statement*) en la cabecera del Curriculum Vitae son competencias indispensables para garantizar una inserción laboral exitosa en entornos internacionales y multiculturales de habla inglesa.

---

<h2 style="color: #29b6f6;">10.1. Introducing Yourself in a Conversation</h2>

Una auto-presentación profesional eficaz y estructurada (*Self-Introduction*) permite transmitir de forma inmediata la identidad, el rol corporativo y el propósito de la interacción. Se compone de una secuencia lógica de tres fases:

### Estructura de la Auto-Presentación Profesional
1. **Greeting (Saludo):** Selección del nivel de formalidad adecuado al contexto (*"Good morning", "Good afternoon", "Hello"*).
2. **Name & Role (Nombre y Puesto):** Declaración clara de la identidad y el cargo técnico o administrativo:  
   *“Hello, my name is [Name], and I am the [Job Title] at [Company Name].”*
3. **Context / Purpose (Contexto y Propósito):** Justificación de la presencia en el evento o exposición breve del área de especialización técnica:  
   *“I specialize in server administration and database maintenance.”*

---

<h2 style="color: #29b6f6;">10.2. Greetings, Saying Goodbye, and Responding to Introductions</h2>

El protocolo conversacional exige dominar las fórmulas de cortesía para dar respuesta a presentaciones de terceros y mantener la fluidez mediante preguntas de seguimiento (*follow-up questions*):

### Repertorio de Respuestas y Fórmulas de Cortesía
* **Respuestas a Introducciones Profesionales:**
    * *“It's a pleasure to meet you, [Name].”*
    * *“Pleased to meet you as well.”*
    * *“Nice to meet you. How is your day going so far?”*
* **Preguntas de Seguimiento (*Follow-up Questions*):**
    * *“What brings you to the conference today?”*
    * *“Could you tell me a bit more about your role at [Company]?”*
    * *“How is the system deployment going on your side?”*
* **Despedidas y Cierre Profesional (*Farewells*):**
    * *Formal:* *“Thank you for your time today. I look forward to working with you.”*
    * *Semiformal / Networking:* *“It was great meeting you! Let's stay in touch via LinkedIn.”*

<h2 style="color: #29b6f6;">10.3. Writing Your Statement for Your CV</h2>

El **Personal Statement** (*o Professional Profile*) actúa como el gancho principal de lectura para el reclutador en la cabecera del Curriculum Vitae. Su arquitectura sintáctica se articula en tres componentes clave:

### Componentes Sintácticos del Personal Statement
1. **Identificación Profesional:** Adjetivo calificativo de alto impacto + Titulación o puesto actual (*“A detail-oriented and highly motivated IT Systems Technician...”*).
2. **Competencias y Experiencia:** Síntesis de las habilidades técnicas clave y los años de práctica en el sector (*“...with strong experience in local network installation, server backup management, and helpdesk support.”*).
3. **Propuesta de Valor / Objetivo:** Declaración explícita del valor que se busca aportar a la compañía contratante (*“Seeking a Helpdesk Specialist role where I can contribute my troubleshooting skills to a dynamic team.”*).

### Adjetivos de Alto Impacto para Perfiles Técnicos
* *Detail-oriented* (Meticuloso / orientado al detalle).
* *Customer-focused* (Enfocado en la atención al usuario).
* *Proactive / Self-motivated* (Proactivo / con iniciativa propia).
* *Problem-solver / Resourceful* (Resolutivo ante incidencias).
* *Adaptable* (Con alta capacidad de adaptación a nuevos entornos tecnológicos).

<h2 style="color: #29b6f6;">🎯 Practical Scenarios & Technical Application</h2>

!!! example "Scenario 1: Professional Self-Introduction at a Tech Fair"
    **Context:** An SMR graduate introduces themselves at a networking event:  
    * *“Good morning. My name is David Carter, and I am a Junior Network Technician at Global Innovations. I specialize in firewall configuration and router setup, and I'm here today to explore new cloud security tools.”*  
    * **Linguistic breakdown:** Three-step self-introduction (*Greeting $\rightarrow$ Name & Role $\rightarrow$ Context/Purpose*); precise professional vocabulary.

!!! example "Scenario 2: CV Personal Statement for an IT Support Role"
    **Context:** Crafting the professional profile summary for an entry-level candidate's CV:  
    * *“A proactive and certified IT Technician with hands-on experience in assembling hardware, managing Active Directory accounts, and resolving tier-1 network tickets. Seeking an Entry-Level System Administrator position to deliver efficient technical support to corporate infrastructure.”*  
    * **Linguistic breakdown:** Structured personal statement combining identity adjective, core technical hard skills, and career objective.

<h2 style="color: #29b6f6;">📌 Apéndice Técnico: Exam Keys (Claves PAC)</h2>

!!! danger "Critical Protocol & Grammatical Rules for Assessment"
    1. **Titles and Surnames in Formal Introductions:**
        * In formal business presentations, use the courtesy title (*Mr., Ms., Dr.*) followed by the **surname** until the interlocutor invites you to use their first name.
            * ❌ *Nice to meet you, Mr. John* $\rightarrow$ ✔️ ***Nice to meet you, Mr. Smith*** (or *Nice to meet you, John* if informal).
    2. **Exact Structure of the Personal Statement:**
        * The standard logical order of the CV introductory profile is:  
          1. **Who you are** (Adjective + Professional profile).  
          2. **What you can do** (Key competencies + tool experience).  
          3. **What you seek/bring** (Job objective in the firm).
    3. **Hard Skills vs. Soft Skills in Self-Introduction:**
        * **Hard Skills** (Mentioned in the context/purpose): *Python programming, Cisco routing, database backup*.
        * **Soft Skills** (Mentioned as identity adjectives): *Detail-oriented, strong communicator, team player*.
    4. **Standard Responses to Formal Introductions:**
        * When greeted with *"How do you do?"*, the traditional formal exam response is to return the exact same formula: ✔️ ***"How do you do?"***.
        * When greeted with *"Pleased to meet you"*, the correct response is: ✔️ ***"Pleased to meet you too / as well"***.
