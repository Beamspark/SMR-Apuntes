### Pregunta 1
Introducing Yourself in a Business Conversation

Review the following professional spoken introduction model:

!!! quote "🎙️ Spoken Introduction Model: IT Systems Technician"
    "Good morning! My name is Alex White, and I am a Systems and Network Technician at TechData Solutions. In my day-to-day work, I am responsible for managing user accounts in Active Directory, configuring local networks, and providing technical support to resolve hardware and software incidents. I am really excited to be here today to discuss our upcoming infrastructure upgrade project. Outside of work, I enjoy building customized PC builds and participating in cybersecurity challenges. It’s a pleasure to meet you. May I ask your name and what you do?"

Which of the following describes the correct structural sequence for a professional self-introduction in a business setting?

<details class="quiz-option correct">
  <summary>A) Polite greeting → State full name & job title → Provide professional context/responsibilities → Mention a relevant interest → Friendly closing question to the other person.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✓ ¡Exacto!</div>
    La estructura formal recomendada incluye: saludo cortés, identificación con cargo y empresa, contexto sobre tareas habituales o motivo del encuentro, un detalle profesional o técnico de interés y una pregunta abierta educada para ceder la palabra al interlocutor.
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>B) Demanding the other person’s credentials first → Complaining about previous employers → Asking personal questions about family life.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    Un saludo profesional nunca debe exigir datos de forma brusca, criticar empleos anteriores ni indagar en aspectos de la vida privada.
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>C) Skipping greetings completely → Reading a full list of software versions installed on personal devices → Leaving without listening to the answer.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    Omitir el saludo y monopolizar la conversación sin escuchar la respuesta del interlocutor deteriora el establecimiento de relaciones de trabajo.
  </div>
</details>

### Pregunta 2
Responding to Introductions: Jessica Lee

Read the introduction statement:

!!! quote "👤 Speaker 1: Jessica Lee (Senior Marketing Manager)"
    "Good morning! My name is Jessica Lee, and I’m the Senior Marketing Manager at GreenTech Solutions. I’ve heard great things about your work, and I’m excited to finally meet you. How are you today?"

What is the most appropriate professional response to Jessica’s introduction?

<details class="quiz-option correct">
  <summary>A) "Good morning, Jessica. It’s a pleasure to meet you as well. I’m doing well, thank you."</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✓ ¡Exacto!</div>
    Devuelve el saludo con el mismo nivel de formalidad, corresponde al cumplido expresando el agrado de conocerla y responde de forma educada y concisa a la pregunta sobre su estado actual (<em>"How are you today?"</em>).
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>B) "Whatever. I don't care what you heard about me."</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    Respuesta agresiva e inaceptable en un registro profesional.
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>C) "I have a lot of personal problems right now, so let me complain for twenty minutes."</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    La pregunta de cortesía <em>"How are you today?"</em> en entornos corporativos exige una fórmula breve y positiva de cortesía, no un desglose de problemas íntimos.
  </div>
</details>

### Pregunta 8
Writing a Personal Statement for a Technical CV

Review the following model personal statement:

!!! quote "📄 Model Personal Statement: IT Systems Technician"
    "A detail-oriented and proactive IT Systems Technician with hands-on experience in local network installation, server backup management, and Active Directory user support. Skilled in troubleshooting hardware issues and configuring router security. Seeking a Helpdesk Specialist position in a dynamic company to deliver efficient technical support and contribute to network optimization."

Which of the following correctly identifies the four indispensable elements that must be included in an effective Personal Statement?

<details class="quiz-option correct">
  <summary>A) Professional title or role + Key technical skills/strengths + Relevant hands-on experience/achievements + Clear career goal/objective.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✓ ¡Exacto!</div>
    Un <em>Personal Statement</em> eficaz debe responder en 3 a 5 líneas a: quién eres profesionalmente (rol/título), qué sabes hacer (habilidades técnicas demostrables), qué has conseguido o manejado (experiencia práctica) y qué aportación buscas realizar en la empresa (meta laboral).
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>B) Marital status + Detailed monthly salary demands + Medical history + Primary school grades.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    Los datos personales íntimos, salarios exigidos y expedientes escolares no deben figurar en el extracto profesional.
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>C) A list of grievances against previous supervisors + Detailed vacation requests for the upcoming year.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    El perfil profesional debe ser constructivo, conciso y centrado exclusivamente en el valor técnico que se aporta al equipo.
  </div>
</details>
