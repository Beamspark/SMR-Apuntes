### Pregunta 5
Determine the correct punctuation regarding commas in each relative clause:

a. London which is my favourite city has many museums.

<details class="quiz-option correct">
  <summary>A) London, which is my favourite city, has many museums.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✓ ¡Exacto!</div>
    <em>London</em> es un nombre propio universalmente identificado; la cláusula es <em>non-defining</em> y debe encerrarse entre dos comas.
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>B) London which is my favourite city has many museums. (No commas needed)</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    Omitir las comas con un nombre propio en una cláusula explicativa constituye un error de puntuación grave.
  </div>
</details>

b. The people who work in this office are very friendly.

<details class="quiz-option correct">
  <summary>A) The people who work in this office are very friendly. (No commas needed)</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✓ ¡Exacto!</div>
    Es una cláusula especificativa (<em>defining</em>): identifica con precisión a qué personas nos referimos (las que trabajan en esta oficina), por lo que no lleva comas.
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>B) The people, who work in this office, are very friendly.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    Poner comas aislaría una información esencial necesaria para determinar el sujeto <em>The people</em>.
  </div>
</details>

c. My brother who lives in Chile is a doctor.

<details class="quiz-option correct">
  <summary>A) My brother, who lives in Chile, is a doctor.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✓ ¡Exacto!</div>
    Al referirse a un familiar directo ya individualizado en el contexto, la residencia en Chile actúa como información explicativa añadida entre comas.
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>B) My brother who lives in Chile is a doctor. (No commas needed)</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    En la normativa estándar de este ejercicio se trata como <em>non-defining</em> al estar el antecedente delimitado.
  </div>
</details>

d. The hotel which we stayed at was near the beach.

<details class="quiz-option correct">
  <summary>A) The hotel which we stayed at was near the beach. (No commas needed)</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✓ ¡Exacto!</div>
    Cláusula <em>defining</em>: restringe e identifica exactamente a qué hotel nos referimos, prescindiendo de comas.
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>B) The hotel, which we stayed at, was near the beach.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    No se deben colocar comas cuando la información es imprescindible para identificar el sustantivo.
  </div>
</details>

e. Mount Everest which is the highest mountain in the world is in Asia.

<details class="quiz-option correct">
  <summary>A) Mount Everest, which is the highest mountain in the world, is in Asia.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✓ ¡Exacto!</div>

### Pregunta 6
Identify and correct the single grammatical mistake in each sentence:

a. The man which helped me was very kind.

<details class="quiz-option correct">
  <summary>A) The man who (or that) helped me was very kind.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✓ ¡Exacto!</div>
    <em>Which</em> solo se utiliza para animales o cosas inanimadas; para seres humanos debe emplearse <strong>who</strong> (o <em>that</em>).
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>B) The man whose helped me was very kind.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    <em>Whose</em> expresa posesión y exige un sustantivo posterior, no un verbo conjugado.
  </div>
</details>

b. She’s the person whose I told you about.

<details class="quiz-option correct">
  <summary>A) She’s the person who (or whom) I told you about.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✓ ¡Exacto!</div>
    <em>Whose</em> indica posesión (<em>cuyo/a</em>); para referirse a la persona de la cual se habló se debe utilizar <strong>who</strong> o <strong>whom</strong>.
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>B) She’s the person which I told you about.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    <em>Which</em> es incompatible con antecedentes referidos a personas.
  </div>
</details>

c. That’s the school where I studied in.

<details class="quiz-option correct">
  <summary>A) That’s the school which / that I studied in (or where I studied).</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✓ ¡Exacto!</div>
    No se puede duplicar el significado locativo combinando el adverbio <em>where</em> con la preposición <em>in</em> al final. O bien se dice <em>where I studied</em>, o bien <em>which / that I studied in</em>.
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>B) That’s the school whose I studied in.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    <em>Whose</em> indica posesión y resulta agramatical en este contexto.
  </div>
</details>

d. My uncle, that lives in Paris, is visiting next month.

<details class="quiz-option correct">
  <summary>A) My uncle, who lives in Paris, is visiting next month.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✓ ¡Exacto!</div>
    En inglés está terminantemente prohibido utilizar el pronombre <strong>that</strong> en cláusulas de relativo explicativas (<em>non-defining</em>, entre comas). Debe emplearse <strong>who</strong>.
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>B) My uncle, which lives in Paris, is visiting next month.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    <em>Which</em> no puede aplicarse a seres humanos.
  </div>
</details>

e. The woman to who you spoke is my aunt.

<details class="quiz-option correct">
  <summary>A) The woman to whom you spoke is my aunt.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✓ ¡Exacto!</div>
    Tras una preposición rectora (<em>to</em>) no puede emplearse <em>who</em>; la gramática normativa impone la forma de objeto <strong>whom</strong> (<em>to whom</em>).
  </div>
</details>
<details class="quiz-option incorrect">
  <summary>B) The woman to that you spoke is my aunt.</summary>
  <div class="feedback">
    <div class="feedback-title">✗ Incorrecto</div>
    <em>That</em> nunca puede ir precedido de preposición en una cláusula de relativo.
  </div>
</details>

What is the fundamental functional difference between a standard cover letter and a **Cold Cover Letter**?

<details class="quiz-option correct">
